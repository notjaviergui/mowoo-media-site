# Realistic cake artwork

Generated using the built-in image generation tool. Asset: `public/images/cake-layer-real.png`. One transparent image is reused for all five layers.

## Generation prompt

Use case: product-mockup. A single round vanilla sponge cake disk, 22cm diameter and 3cm tall, with 8mm ivory buttercream. Photorealistic porous golden sponge, natural crumbs, soft frosting and subtle spatula swirls. Low three-quarter studio product photography, level horizontal disk, entire cake visible. Transparent background. Soft upper-left key light and right edge light for a near-black website. No plate, additional layers, text, decorations or hands. Avoid metal, plastic, illustration or vector appearance.

## Motion

Scroll arms each layer. A finite animation-frame simulation applies downward acceleration, collision at its resting position, 22% bounce restitution and a small damped compression at impact. The next layer starts only after the previous has settled. Scrolling back above a step resets its layer. Reduced-motion preference displays the full stack without movement.
